# Basic, Advanced, and the learning tutor

Both versions are free and edit the same project. Switching versions changes
the controls on screen; it does not convert, flatten, or replace your song.

## First visit

Choose **Basic** if you are learning music software. Choose **Advanced** for
the full timeline, instruments, recording, mixer, MIDI, and sound controls.
The separate **Learning tutor** choice appears at welcome and at the top of
the studio. It works in either version and can be turned off at any time.

Pick a starter song, **Blank Project**, or **Keep my project**. Before a welcome
template opens, the studio preserves the current project. If preservation
fails, your current project stays open and an error explains what happened.

## Basic: your first beat or song

1. Start with your headphones or speakers at a comfortable volume. Click
   **Tap to Enable Audio**.
2. In **Make your beat**, choose **Add starter beat**, or **Draw a new beat**
   and tap squares yourself. Kick is the low thump, snare the snap, and hi-hat
   the tick. A checked square plays; tap again to remove it.
3. Press **Play**. Change **Speed** to try slower or faster music. **Pause**
   pauses, **Stop** returns to the beginning, and **Panic** stops every sound.
   **Repeat** switches looping on/off. **Loop this clip** repeats its bars
   together with the other tracks at the same position.
4. In **Add bass and a melody**, add a bass idea and a melody idea. Starting
   with a new beat places these parts in the same new section wherever their
   tracks have room. Choose a track and **Clip to edit**, then tap notes to
   change the tune. Use the bar arrows to edit another bar.
5. In **Make it a song**, use **Repeat song** to append a copy. Repeated clicks
   append the original captured section once each instead of doubling the
   whole arrangement. Edit the copied clips for a different ending. Use
   **Loop whole song** to hear everything together.
6. In **Balance your sounds**, try a different sound and adjust each track's
   volume. Mute a part and bring it back. If a track was soloed in Advanced,
   **Hear all** lets the other tracks play again.
7. Name your project at the top, then **Save project**. Wait for **Project
   saved**. **Load** opens projects saved in this browser or a downloaded
   backup through **Open project backup (JSON)**.
8. **Export / download** opens WAV, MP3, and project JSON backup choices.
   WAV/MP3 are for listening. A JSON backup keeps the editable project and
   imported samples. Keep a downloaded backup: clearing browser data can
   remove local saves. To reopen one, use **Load → Open project backup (JSON)**,
   choose your file, review its contents, then confirm **Replace project**.
   Extra formats remain in **Advanced export options**.

**Undo latest addition** works until another project change and restores the
previous music and selection. Reverse a grid edit by tapping the same square.
Basic preserves notes its grid cannot display, samples, recordings, and
advanced sound settings. Use Advanced for off-grid timing, other drums, note
lengths/velocities, recording, clip movement, and automation. Phone grids scroll
sideways inside their cards without widening the page.

## Advanced: the full workflow

| Task | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Start or open music | Project menu, Load projects/demos, JSON/MIDI import, sound packs |
| Play and record | Transport, tempo, metronome, count-in, loop; select and arm a track |
| Choose sounds | Tracks/Sounds browser and selected instrument presets |
| Edit notes and samples | Drum Pads grid, piano roll, keyboard/guitar panels, sample instruments, Chop Lab |
| Arrange | Timeline clip dragging, resizing, copying and duplication, sections |
| Mix | Mixer/Channel Strips, EQ, sends, effects rack, master controls |
| Connect a controller | MIDI panel, device selection, MIDI Learn, performance controls |
| Learn | Tutor toggle, Learn → Lessons, The Dojo, glossary |
| Keep and export | Save/Save As, project backups, WAV/MP3, additional export formats |
| Troubleshoot | Panic, audio diagnostics, Settings → Performance Mode |

Reopen collapsed Tracks, Inspector, and Mixer panels from their rails.
Tablets and phones use drawers or instrument tabs for existing mobile tools.
Basic/Advanced controls remain reachable on small screens.
The phone tutor offers **Practice in Basic** for note-grid and arrangement
lessons; the full Advanced Inspector, piano roll, and timeline need a larger
screen.

## Optional tutor

Use the studio while reading this nonmodal, self-paced guide. Seven short
lessons cover the studio, rhythm, listening/tempo, melody, arrangement, mixing,
and saving/exporting. **Next lesson** and **Back** let you learn at your own
pace; advancing is not a graded test or a claim that an export succeeded.
Task buttons lead to a Basic section, play your music, or open export. Audio
readiness and successful Basic edits give feedback.

**Tutor off** removes the guide. **Tutor on** resumes your place after reload
or mode change. **Restart tutor** appears after finishing and in Settings.
Restarting only changes learning progress. Existing Advanced users keep their
chosen view and start with the tutor off unless they enable it.

The guide runs locally and requires no account, AI service, microphone, or
personal information. Learn → Lessons remains a reference library, and The
Dojo offers deeper musical ideas when requested.

## Keyboard basics

Space plays/pauses; Enter stops; Esc is Panic. S saves; B opens export; ? opens
shortcuts. Typing fields keep normal typing behavior. Focused buttons keep
Space/Enter activation, and dialogs own their keyboard actions.
Delete/Backspace do not remove whole clips in Basic. Switch to the Advanced
timeline when you intend to delete a selected clip.
